# Implementation timeline

This file lays out when EHDS obligations become mandatory. See the [README](README.md) for methodology caveats. As noted throughout, **direct WebFetch access to eur-lex.europa.eu and most law-firm/industry sites was blocked** during research; all dates below are drawn from WebSearch-tool summaries of secondary/tertiary sources (including quoted snippets purporting to reflect Article 105's text), not independently verified primary-text reads. A follow-up verification pass against the consolidated EUR-Lex text ([https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/327/oj/eng](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/327/oj/eng)) is recommended before relying on any specific date or article number.

## Phasing logic

The Regulation follows a **four-stage rollout keyed to entry-into-force-plus-N-years**: +2 years (2027, general application/governance groundwork), +4 years (2029, first data-category tranche plus most secondary-use rules), +6 years (2031, second data-category tranche plus remaining secondary-use categories), +10 years (2035, the narrow third-country/international-organisation participation provision, Article 75(5)). The commonly cited "2027/2029/2031" cadence in press coverage refers to this +2/+4/+6-year pattern from the March 2025 entry into force, with the 2035 milestone being less commonly reported.

Quoted/paraphrased text attributed to **Article 105 (Entry into force and application)**: "Articles 3 to 15, Article 23(2) to (6), Articles 25, 26, 27, 47, 48 and 49 shall apply from 26 March 2029 to priority categories of personal electronic health data referred to in Article 14(1), points (a), (b) and (c), and to EHR systems intended by the manufacturer to process such categories of data; and from 26 March 2031 to priority categories of personal electronic health data referred to in Article 14(1), points (d), (e) and (f), and to EHR systems intended by the manufacturer to process such categories of data" ([StreamLex Art. 105 via WebSearch synthesis](https://streamlex.eu/articles/ehds-en-art-105/)). Article 75(12) (a HealthData@EU-related provision) applies from 26 March 2027, but "implementing acts referred to in Article 75(12) shall apply from 26 March 2029" ([StreamLex Art. 75](https://streamlex.eu/articles/ehds-en-art-75/)).

A Dutch legal-timeline resource frames the milestones as: 2025 entry into force; by 26 March 2027 Member States/Commission complete preparatory steps (designating supervisory authorities, Commission adopting secondary legislation on ICT system requirements); 26 March 2029 Priority Category 1 data plus Chapter IV (secondary use) become mandatory and HDABs must be operational; 26 March 2031 Priority Category 2 and "Chapter III (EHR systems in service)" become mandatory; 26 March 2035 final transitional provisions complete ([ehds-jurist.nl](https://ehds-jurist.nl/en/tijdlijn)).

### Primary use: no confirmed "sending vs. receiving" split

No source found in this research distinguishes a separate "sending" obligation deadline from a "receiving" obligation deadline for Member States under MyHealth@EU. This is an open question — it should be checked directly against Articles 3–15 and Article 23 of the consolidated text rather than assumed either way.

### Secondary use: data-holder response timelines

Member States must have designated their HDABs and related supervisory structures by around 26 March 2027, with HDABs required to be fully operational by 26 March 2029 ([GA4GH policy brief](https://www.ga4gh.org/news_item/the-european-health-data-space-from-approval-to-national-implementation/); [ehds-jurist.nl](https://ehds-jurist.nl/en/tijdlijn)). Data holders must generally respond to/fulfil health data access requests **within three months** (extendable in exceptional circumstances), and must submit dataset descriptions starting 26 March 2029 ([Bird & Bird BioTalk](https://biotalk.twobirds.com/post/102k70n/health-data-holders-obligations-under-the-ehds-what-when-and-how-will-we-know); [EPF analysis PDF](https://www.eu-patient.eu/globalassets/ehds-analysis---final.pdf)). As of 26 March 2029, data access requests can be made at any time for most categories; for some categories (e.g., genomic data) such requests can only be made from 26 March 2031 (search synthesis citing [Arnold & Porter](https://www.arnoldporter.com/en/perspectives/advisories/2025/03/european-health-data-space-regulation-published)).

**Microenterprises** (fewer than 10 employees, turnover/balance sheet under €2 million) are generally exempt from health-data-holder obligations, though Member States can override this exemption nationally ([AKD](https://www.akd.eu/insights/european-health-data-space-regulation-cross-border-access-to-health-data)). No separate delayed compliance deadline was found for small (non-micro) or medium enterprises — the SME accommodation appears structured as a scope exemption rather than a staggered compliance timetable, unlike some other EU digital/data regulations (e.g., the AI Act) that use explicit extra-time provisions for SMEs.

### EHR system conformity timeline and the grandfathering question

EHR system manufacturers face the conformity self-assessment/CE-marking regime under Chapter II (placing on the market) and Chapter III (systems in service), with Annex II essential requirements becoming applicable on the same category-based schedule as primary use: **2029** for systems intended to process Priority Category 1 data, **2031** for Priority Category 2 ([Timelex](https://www.timelex.eu/en/blog/navigating-ehds-understanding-electronic-health-record-systems-requirements)). By **26 March 2027**, the Commission must adopt common specifications (implementing acts) for Annex II essential requirements, including a common template and a time limit for implementing them ([StreamLex Art. 36](https://streamlex.eu/articles/ehds-en-art-36/)).

> ⚠️ **Unverified / conflicting sources**: No source explicitly confirmed or denied a formal "grandfathering" clause exempting EHR systems already on the market before the relevant application date from retroactive compliance. One source's framing of "Chapter III (EHR systems in service)" becoming mandatory only from 2031 hints at a de facto transition period for legacy systems, but this reading is an inference, not a confirmed provision ([Baker McKenzie](https://healthcarelifesciences.bakermckenzie.com/2024/11/04/ehds-deep-dive-ehr-systems-wellness-apps-and-medical-devices-part-4/), which itself states it could not find specific grandfathering information).

## Commission implementing/delegated acts: what is still pending

Beyond the two implementing regulations already adopted (EHDS Board — April 2026; MyHealth@EU and HealthDCAT-AP — September/October 2026), the following are described as still pending or in progress as of the most recent findings (September 2026):

- **Common specifications for Annex II essential requirements** (EHR system interoperability/security requirements), statutory deadline **26 March 2027**, via implementing acts under Article 36 ([StreamLex Art. 36](https://streamlex.eu/articles/ehds-en-art-36/)).
- **Technical specifications for the EEHRxF itself** — see [05-gateway-market-and-api-status.md](05-gateway-market-and-api-status.md) for the detailed standards-status discussion; the EEHRxF is described as "not yet formally established" and expected only around March 2027.
- **Detailed requirements for EHR manufacturers registering systems** in the EU database, and **security measures for secure processing environments** ([noze.it](https://www.noze.it/en/insights/ehds-regulation-2025-327/)).
- An **"Implementing Act EHDS: Identity Management"**, referenced as upcoming/in-progress by Bitkom (German digital industry association) as of 2026 ([Bitkom](https://www.bitkom.org/EN/List-and-detailpages/Publications/Implementing-Act-EHDS-Identity-Management)).
- The Commission is separately empowered to adopt **delegated acts amending Annex I** (the priority categories of personal electronic health data) to adapt them to technical developments and international standards (search synthesis citing the [European Commission's general implementing/delegated acts process page](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-making-process/adopting-eu-law/implementing-and-delegated-acts_en); specific article number not confirmed).
- As of early 2026, "negotiations on [the EHDS's] technical elaboration are still ongoing... including... the specific exchange formats for electronic health data," with a legislative proposal intended for public consultation in the first half of 2026 (search synthesis, apparently drawing on [ictrecht.nl](https://www.ictrecht.nl/en/blog/de-implementatie-van-de-ehds-waar-staan-we-begin-2026)).

The Commission appears to be sequencing implementing acts in dependency order: governance body first (EHDS Board, April 2026), then core infrastructure regulations (MyHealth@EU, HealthDCAT-AP, September/October 2026), with technical specifications (identity management, EEHRxF content, Annex II common specifications) still pending as of September 2026. Given the Annex II common-specifications deadline is 26 March 2027, and several other implementing acts were still in progress as of September 2026, there is a plausible risk the Commission will need to compress remaining rulemaking into the final months before the deadline — though no source explicitly declared a missed or at-risk deadline.

> ⚠️ **Unverified / conflicting sources**: No complete, definitive enumeration of all outstanding implementing/delegated acts and their individual statutory deadlines was found; a full list would require direct review of the consolidated Regulation's empowerment articles.

## Delays, extensions, or acceleration (2025–2026 status)

As of the most recent findings (September 2026), EHDS implementation is proceeding roughly on its statutory schedule; **no source found reports a formal amendment to the Regulation's statutory dates** (unlike, for comparison, the unrelated EU Deforestation Regulation's one-year delay, which surfaced as an unrelated search result). What commentary does flag is significant **unevenness in Member State readiness**: "Several Member States, including Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, and Slovakia, show lower eHealth maturity, limited interoperability and governance frameworks, while countries such as Finland and Estonia enter the EHDS era with mature digital health ecosystems" ([GLOBSEC commentary, "Running Before Some of Us Can Walk"](https://www.globsec.org/what-we-do/commentaries/running-some-us-can-walk-implementing-european-health-data-space-ehds)). The same commentary notes: "With the adoption of the first Implementing Act in April 2026, which established the EHDS Board, the implementation marathon has truly begun."

The relatively late (2026) adoption of foundational implementing acts relative to the 2025 entry into force suggests the "preparatory" 2025–2027 window is being used close to fully, increasing execution risk for HIS/EMR vendors and healthcare providers who need finalized technical specifications well before the 2029 deadline.
